If you live in South Korea, or you play on a Korean PlayStation or Xbox account, Grand Theft Auto VI reaches you earlier than it reaches most of the world. Rockstar Games released GTA 6 on Thursday 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Korean accounts unlocked at midnight local time, fourteen hours before the US East Coast. This guide covers the exact unlock time, what the game costs in Korean won, how much storage you need, what Korean-language support actually means, why the game is restricted to adults in Korea, and what is still unknown, including the PC version.

When GTA 6 unlocks in South Korea

GTA 6 does not switch on everywhere at the same moment. It unlocks at midnight local time, region by region, on 19 November. PlayStation Store listings showed New Zealand going first, followed by Australia, with South Korea and Japan in the third wave at midnight KST. That is 15:00 UTC on Wednesday 18 November.

For comparison, the US East Coast unlocks at midnight Eastern Time on 19 November, which is 2:00 pm the same day in Seoul. Players on the US West Coast wait three more hours. In practical terms, someone in Seoul can have been playing for most of a working day before New York gets in.

On PS5, the unlock follows the region attached to your PlayStation Network account, not where you physically are. If you are an expat in Seoul still using a UK or US account, your game unlocks on that country's clock. Microsoft has not published a region-by-region table for Xbox, so Xbox players should treat any specific time other than local midnight as unconfirmed.

Why staggered midnight launches exist

A single worldwide unlock always lands at an awkward hour somewhere. Pick midnight in New York and Korea gets the game at 2:00 pm on a weekday; pick midnight in London and Los Angeles gets it at 4:00 pm the day before. A local-midnight rollout gives every market the same moment, the start of release day, at the cost of some regions waiting longer in absolute time. It also spreads server load across roughly a day instead of concentrating every download and login into one hour.

What GTA 6 costs in Korea

On the Korean PlayStation Store, the Standard Edition is ₩89,800 and the Ultimate Edition is ₩112,800, a gap of ₩23,000. The Ultimate Edition adds in-game extras such as vehicles, weapons and outfits; the story content is the same in both. In the United States the same editions cost $79.99 and $99.99.

The headline Korean price looks low next to the US one, and there are two reasons. US store prices are shown before sales tax, while Korean prices already include 10 percent VAT. Publishers also set regional prices market by market rather than converting at the exchange rate. The Korean Xbox Store price could not be confirmed in two independent reports, so check the checkout screen yourself if you play on Xbox.

Boxed copies reportedly contain a download code rather than a disc. Buying physical does not save you the download.

Storage and the preload

Rockstar's guidance is that the preload needs up to 212 GB, depending on the device. The final installed size can differ from the download size, and a day-one update can add more. A sensible target is to clear around 230 GB before you start.

Preloading opened on 10 November, two days earlier than the 12 November date first announced. In Korean time, the PS5 preload opened at midnight on 10 November and the Xbox preload at 9:00 am, because Xbox used a single global start at midnight GMT. If you buy now, the full download starts straight away. At a steady 100 Mbps, 212 GB takes close to five hours, so start it before you go to bed rather than at 11:55 pm.

Korean language support: subtitles, not dubbing

Korean is one of the 13 languages GTA 6 supports at launch, and the Korean PlayStation Store page lists it. That support covers menus, on-screen text and subtitles. Several outlets report that spoken dialogue is in English only, but we could not find the official store wording on voice languages, so we treat the voice-language point as unconfirmed.

If the game boots in English on a console set to Korean, check the system language first. Rockstar has not confirmed whether GTA 6 has its own in-game language menu, so that detail is also unconfirmed.

Why GTA 6 is rated adults only in Korea

The Korean rating was set by the Game Contents Rating Board (GCRB), a private classification body, rather than the government's Game Rating and Administration Committee. The GCRB classified GTA 6 as adults only at a meeting on 16 October 2026. Korean reports cited the game's violence, sexual content and depictions of drug use.

The decision was not a formality. At the first review in September, committee members reportedly split three to three between an adults-only rating and a refusal to classify. A refusal would have blocked a legal Korean release altogether. Elsewhere, the game is rated M by the ESRB in North America and 18 by PEGI in Europe.

In practice, the Korean rating means accounts registered to anyone under 19 cannot buy or play the game. If you share a console with younger family members, it is worth checking the parental-control settings on their accounts before release night.

How we got here: two delays

Rockstar first gave a release window of fall 2025. In May 2025 it moved the date to 26 May 2026. In November 2025, alongside Take-Two Interactive's earnings report, it moved the date again to 19 November 2026, and Take-Two's share price fell sharply in after-hours trading. Early in November 2026, Rockstar said the date was holding. GTA 5 came out in 2013, so this is the first new mainline entry in thirteen years.

Reviews went live on 18 November. PC Gamer's roundup and The National's summary both highlighted the relationship between the two leads, Jason and Lucia, and the detail of Leonida, the game's fictional take on Florida. Aggregate review scores varied between early counts, so we have not quoted one.

Is there a PC version?

Not yet. The 19 November release covers PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and Rockstar has not given a PC date. Its stated reasoning is that console players make up its core audience. Any specific PC date you see, such as early 2027 or 2028, comes from leaks or speculation.

Rockstar has a habit of releasing on console first. Red Dead Redemption 2 reached PC about 13 months after its console launch, and GTA 5 took about 19 months. Those gaps are history, not a forecast for GTA 6.

What to do tonight

  • Open your console's storage screen and free up about 230 GB.
  • Check that your account region is the one you expect, because that decides your unlock time.
  • Confirm the account's date of birth shows you as 19 or older if you are buying in Korea.
  • Pick Standard unless you specifically want the Ultimate cosmetics; the story is identical.
  • If you only play on PC, hold off and watch Rockstar's official channels.
