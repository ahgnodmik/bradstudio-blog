If you are in South Korea between now and mid-November, you will see "Korea Grand Festival" and "K-Sale Festa" banners in department stores, supermarkets, and shopping apps. The posters promise discounts of up to 50%, but it is not obvious which deals a tourist or a foreign resident can actually use.

This guide sorts the festival into three groups: benefits aimed at foreign visitors, sales that anyone can walk into, and programs whose eligibility for non-Koreans has not been confirmed. Dates below come from reports by at least two independent Korean news outlets unless marked otherwise.

What the Korea Grand Festival is

The Korea Grand Festival is a government-led consumption campaign that bundles several separate discount events under one name. The government announced the second edition on October 27, 2026. It runs for 18 days, from Thursday, October 29 to Sunday, November 15, 2026, and around 40,000 companies and stores are taking part, according to multiple reports.

The main pieces are:

  • K-Sale Festa, the retail and manufacturing sale formerly known as the Korea Sale Festa. Department stores, supermarkets, duty-free shops, online malls, carmakers, and appliance makers join in.
  • Donghaeng Festival, run by the Ministry of SMEs and Startups, which promotes small businesses and traditional markets.
  • Food discounts on Korean beef and other farm products, led by the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs, and seafood sales led by the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries.
  • Tourism and culture programs, including free palace admission and duty-free promotions for foreign visitors, with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism involved.

"Up to 50%" is a ceiling for selected items, not a typical discount. Most products will be marked down far less, and each retailer sets its own terms.

For visitors: free palaces and duty-free deals

Free palace admission

From October 29 to November 15, admission is free at Seoul's four main royal palaces: Gyeongbokgung, Changdeokgung, Deoksugung, and Changgyeonggung. Jongmyo Shrine and the Joseon royal tombs are also free during the same period. Two exceptions matter: the Secret Garden at Changdeokgung is not included, and each site keeps its regular closing day. Gyeongbokgung, for example, is normally closed on Tuesdays. Late autumn also brings earlier closing times, so check hours on the day.

Free admission usually means bigger crowds. If you want photos without a wall of people, arrive at opening time on a weekday.

Duty-free discounts

Twenty-five duty-free shops nationwide are offering discounts of up to 25% on items such as cosmetics and liquor during a welcome week for foreign tourists from October 30 to November 15. The usual duty-free rules still apply: bring your passport, and for airport pickup items, your flight details. Compare the festival price with the shop's regular member price and any app coupon, since stacking rules differ between chains.

Other travel perks

Korea JoongAng Daily reported a 25% discount on portable Wi-Fi rental for foreign tourists and a 2,000-won discount on Airport Railroad Express (AREX) trains during the festival. We found these details in only one English-language report, so treat them as unconfirmed and check with the rental counter or AREX before relying on them.

For everyone: in-store sales

The simplest way to benefit is to shop where the signs are. Supermarkets and department stores apply K-Sale Festa prices at the till, with no registration needed. Hanwoo, Korea's premium native beef, is one of the headline items: participating retailers are selling it at up to 50% off from October 28 to November 8. If you are staying somewhere with a kitchen, this is a rare chance to try it at a lower price.

Online malls are also participating. Many Korean shopping apps require a Korean phone number for sign-up, so visitors may find in-person stores easier.

For foreign residents: cars and appliances

Five Korean automakers (Hyundai, Kia, GM Korea, Renault Korea, and KG Mobility) are joining from November, and Samsung Electronics and LG Electronics are taking part on the appliance side. Reports say certified used cars from Hyundai and Kia are included for the first time. Discounts by model had not been fully published by all companies at the time of writing.

If you hold an Alien Registration Card and plan to buy a car or a large appliance anyway, the festival can be a useful moment to ask for quotes. Ask the dealer whether the festival discount stacks with existing promotions, and confirm the delivery date, since some promotions apply only to vehicles delivered within a set window.

Digital Onnuri gift certificates: the unconfirmed part

Onnuri gift certificates are vouchers that can be spent at traditional markets and registered small shops. The digital version is bought through the Digital Onnuri app. From Monday, November 2 to Friday, November 6, the purchase discount rises from the usual 7% to 10%, according to several reports.

Korean reports also describe a "win-win consumption lottery" with a total prize pool of 1.7 billion won, running from October 15 to November 15 for people who pay with Digital Onnuri certificates. Korean coverage says you must agree to the lottery in the app, and that offline payments earn entries in 30,000-won steps.

Can foreigners use this? We could not confirm it. None of the festival reports we checked said whether foreign residents or tourists can buy Digital Onnuri certificates or enter the lottery. Buying through the app involves identity verification and linking a Korean bank account or card. A foreign resident with an Alien Registration Card, a phone in their own name, and a Korean bank account may be able to complete those steps, but that is an assumption, not an official rule. Short-term tourists without a Korean phone number and bank account are unlikely to get through sign-up. Check the Digital Onnuri app or the Small Enterprise and Market Service before planning around this discount.

Practical tips

  • Check the real price, not the discount. Compare with what the item cost last week rather than the list price.
  • Ask about tax refunds. Visitors can often claim back VAT on qualifying purchases at stores showing a tax-free sign. Keep receipts and carry your passport.
  • Watch the end dates. Beef deals end November 8. The festival ends November 15.
  • Expect crowds at palaces on weekends. Plan visits for weekday mornings.

Still unconfirmed

  • Whether foreign residents or tourists can buy Digital Onnuri gift certificates or enter the lottery.
  • The portable Wi-Fi and AREX discounts, found in a single report.
  • Model-by-model car and appliance discounts for all participating brands.
  • The exact date of the official opening ceremony, which reports gave differently.

Before November 15, pick one palace and one shopping trip. Visit the palace on a weekday morning with no ticket needed, and if you are a resident, open the Digital Onnuri app on November 2 to see whether your account can complete verification.
