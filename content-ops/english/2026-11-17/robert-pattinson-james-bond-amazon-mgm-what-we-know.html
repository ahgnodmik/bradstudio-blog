Robert Pattinson is the next James Bond. Amazon MGM Studios made it official on Monday 16 November 2026, ending a search that had run for more than five years since Daniel Craig's last outing. If you are trying to separate what has actually been announced from the flood of speculation that will follow, this guide lays out the confirmed facts, the open questions, and the dates worth watching.

What Amazon MGM announced

Amazon MGM Studios named Pattinson as the new 007 on 16 November, local time. The announcement appeared on Amazon's own newsroom and was reported the same day by CNBC, CNN, Deadline and Variety. The film will be the 26th entry in the official series and the first Bond picture made since Amazon MGM took creative control of the franchise.

The creative team around him was already in place before the casting:

  • Director: Denis Villeneuve, the filmmaker behind the Dune films and Blade Runner 2049. His hiring was first reported in June 2025 by Variety and NBC News.
  • Screenwriter: Steven Knight, creator of Peaky Blinders. Amazon confirmed him as writer in July 2025.
  • Producers: Amy Pascal and David Heyman, according to Empire and Yahoo Entertainment.

Pattinson called the role "the honour of a lifetime" in his statement, a line carried by CNBC, CNN and Ireland's public broadcaster RTÉ. Villeneuve thanked the actors who auditioned and, according to CNN and TheWrap, made the point that there can only be one 007.

How the casting race played out

A month earlier, Pattinson was one name on a list. The Hollywood Reporter was first to report that he had screen-tested in London over the weekend of 24 October, while he was in the city filming The Batman Part II. TheWrap and others followed. In early November, Variety and Deadline reported that Nicholas Hoult had also tested.

Other names repeatedly linked to the role in coverage from both outlets were Callum Turner, Jack Lowden and Josh O'Connor. Amazon MGM did not comment on any of the candidates while tests were going on. The gap between the first screen-test report and the official announcement was roughly three weeks.

Why Pattinson? The studio has not published selection criteria. One link is obvious, though: he already works with Villeneuve. Pattinson appears in Dune: Part Three, and Deadline pointed to that collaboration as part of the background to the decision. Treat that as context rather than a stated reason.

Why Amazon, not Eon, made this call

For decades, Bond casting was decided by Eon Productions under Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson. That changed in February 2025, when Amazon MGM set up a joint venture with the pair and took over creative control of the franchise. Broccoli and Wilson remain co-owners, according to Fortune and Variety, but decisions on casting and story now sit with Amazon.

The price drew attention. Early estimates put the value of the deal at around $1 billion. Then, in October 2025, a UK filing by Eon showed a figure of $20 million, as reported by Screen Daily and GamesRadar+. Analysts noted that other terms, such as equity or future profit-sharing, may not appear in that filing, and Amazon MGM declined to comment. The full economic value of the deal is therefore unconfirmed.

Is he the seventh or the eighth Bond?

You will see both numbers. Al Jazeera and most Korean outlets call Pattinson the seventh Bond, counting the six actors who led the official Eon series from Sean Connery to Daniel Craig. The Hollywood Reporter counted him as the eighth, which appears to include an actor who played Bond outside the official series. Neither count is wrong; they simply use different definitions.

For context, Craig began with Casino Royale in 2006 and bowed out with No Time to Die in 2021, according to the Motion Picture Association and ABC7. That makes this the longest stretch in recent memory without a named successor.

When will Bond 26 be released?

There is no release date. RTÉ and CNBC both describe a production expected to start in 2027 with a release in 2028, but Amazon MGM has not given dates. Some older reports pointed to late 2027; those appear to predate the current schedule. Until the studio publishes a date, any specific month you see is a projection.

Two scheduling factors are worth tracking. First, the rest of the cast has to be assembled, and Deadline reports that is the next step. Second, Pattinson is also committed to The Batman Part II, and how the two shoots are sequenced has not been announced.

The Korea connection

Korean audiences get an early look at the Villeneuve and Pattinson partnership. Warner Bros. Korea announced that Dune: Part Three opens in South Korea on 16 December 2026, the first release anywhere and two days ahead of the North American date of 18 December, according to Special Times and Vogue Korea (both in Korean).

Korean media also framed the casting through Pattinson's work with director Bong Joon Ho. He played the dual role of Mickey 17 and Mickey 18 in Mickey 17, which opened in South Korea on 28 February 2025 ahead of its US release, as reported by the Kyunghyang Shinmun and The Fact (both in Korean). That film widened his fan base in Korea beyond Twilight and The Batman.

How Bond 26 will reach Korean screens is unknown. No local distributor has been named, and Amazon MGM has not said how long the film will stay in cinemas before moving to streaming.

How to follow the story without falling for rumors

  • Check the source chain. If ten sites carry the same claim but all cite one outlet, it is still one source. The three-picture deal report is a current example.
  • Watch the wording. "Expected," "reportedly" and "eyeing" signal projections, not announcements.
  • Be skeptical of trailers. The film has not started shooting. Any "trailer" showing Pattinson in a tuxedo is fan-made or edited from other footage.
  • Go to the studio. Amazon's newsroom and the official 007 channels are where dates and the title will appear first.

If you want an early read on what this partnership looks like on screen, book a seat for Dune: Part Three, opening 16 December in Korea and 18 December in North America. Then set a news alert for "Bond 26" and wait for the studio, not the rumor mill, to name the date.
