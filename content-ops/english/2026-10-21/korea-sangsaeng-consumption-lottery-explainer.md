# Korea's Sangsaeng Consumption Lottery: How Market Shopping Can Win You KRW 30 Million

You shop at a Korean market anyway, and you want to know whether the government's new lottery is worth five minutes of setup.

The short answer is yes, if you already pay with Digital Onnuri. The Ministry of SMEs and Startups is running the Sangsaeng Consumption Lottery (Sangsaeng Sobi Bokgwon) from October 15 to November 15, 2026. It turns everyday spending at local shops into lottery entries. The prize pool is KRW 1.7 billion, split among 5,600 winners. This guide explains the rules, the payment app behind it, and why the government runs it.

## What the lottery is

"Sangsaeng" means "mutual prosperity" or "win-win." The idea is simple. You spend at small businesses. The government rewards you with a chance at a prize.

Here are the core facts:

- Organizer: Ministry of SMEs and Startups
- Period: October 15 to November 15, 2026
- Condition: cumulative spending of KRW 30,000 or more with Digital Onnuri at offline member stores
- Winners: 5,600 people in total
- Prize pool: KRW 1.7 billion

Only payments made with the Digital Onnuri app count. Online purchases do not count. Paper gift certificates do not count. Last year's version was based on credit card spending, so older guides you find online describe the wrong rules. Check that any article you read refers to 2026.

## What Onnuri gift certificates are

Onnuri gift certificates (Onnuri Sangpumgwon) are government-backed vouchers. You use them like cash at traditional markets and at registered small shops.

They come in two main forms:

- Paper certificates
- Digital Onnuri, a mobile app that lets you buy credit and pay by QR code or card at member stores

The ministry often sells them at a discount, especially before Lunar New Year and Chuseok. Local governments also run cashback events that pay shoppers back in Onnuri certificates. Seoul did this for Chuseok 2026 at dozens of traditional markets.

You cannot use them everywhere. Only registered member stores accept them. The Digital Onnuri app has a map of member stores near you.

## Why the government does this

Traditional markets and small merchants compete with large retailers and online shopping. Onnuri certificates exist to push spending back toward these markets and small merchants.

This lottery adds a second goal: regional balance. Korea's economy is heavily concentrated in the capital region, which means Seoul, Incheon, and Gyeonggi Province. The government wants more money to flow to the rest of the country. The lottery rules reflect that directly, as the next sections show.

The prizes also stay in the system. Winners receive their money as Digital Onnuri credit, not cash. That means the prize money gets spent again at member stores.

## How entries work

Your entries depend on where you spend, not where you live.

- Capital region stores: 1 entry per KRW 30,000
- Stores outside the capital region: 2 entries per KRW 30,000
- Maximum: 30 entries per person

Here is a simple example. You spend KRW 90,000 at a market in Seoul. You get 3 entries. You spend the same KRW 90,000 at a market in Busan or Jeonju. You get 6 entries.

To reach the 30-entry cap, you need KRW 450,000 of spending outside the capital region. In the capital region, you need KRW 900,000.

## The prize structure

There are four prize tiers:

- 1st prize: 10 winners, KRW 30 million each
- 2nd prize: 40 winners, KRW 2 million each
- 3rd prize: 850 winners, KRW 1 million each
- 4th prize: 4,700 winners, KRW 100,000 each

The four tiers add up to 5,600 winners and KRW 1.7 billion. Most winners land in the 4th tier.

One rule matters most. The 1st prize goes only to entries earned outside the capital region. If all your entries come from Seoul stores, you cannot win KRW 30 million. You can still win 2nd, 3rd, or 4th prize. Those tiers are drawn from all participants nationwide.

A Seoul resident can still qualify for 1st prize. You just need to spend with Digital Onnuri at a member store outside the capital region during the event. A weekend trip to a regional market would do it.

## How to take part

You do not press a separate entry button. You open the Digital Onnuri app and agree to share your personal information for the event. After that, entries build up automatically from your payments.

Reports say you can give consent any time up to November 15. Payments from October 15 onward still count. Do not leave it to the last day. It is easy to forget.

## Winners and payment

- Notification: early December, through the Digital Onnuri app
- Prize payment: in December
- Form of payment: Digital Onnuri credit, not cash

The exact announcement date has not been published yet.

## Can foreign residents take part?

This is the question many readers will have. We could not confirm it.

We searched for clear guidance on whether foreign residents can sign up for Digital Onnuri, for example with an alien registration card and phone identity verification. We did not find two independent sources that confirm the rules. Eligibility for foreign residents is therefore unconfirmed.

In practice, many Korean apps require identity verification through a phone line registered in your own name. If your phone plan is under your name and you hold an alien registration card, you may be able to sign up. Do not assume it. Check inside the Digital Onnuri app or ask its customer center before you plan spending around the lottery.

## Things to keep in mind

- KRW 1.7 billion is the total prize pool, not what one person wins.
- More entries raise your odds. They do not guarantee a prize.
- Do not spend money you would not spend anyway. A KRW 100,000 4th prize does not cover extra shopping.
- Ignore text messages with links that claim to be about the lottery. Take part only through the official Digital Onnuri app.
- Details on refunds, cancelled payments, and the exact draw date are still pending. Watch for official notices.

## What to do before November 15

1. Install the Digital Onnuri app and check whether you can complete identity verification.
2. Open the event page in the app and agree to the personal information consent.
3. Top up your Digital Onnuri balance with what you would normally spend on groceries.
4. Pay at offline member stores. Use the store map in the app.
5. If you travel outside Seoul, Incheon, or Gyeonggi before November 15, pay at a local market there. Those payments earn double entries and make you eligible for 1st prize.
6. Keep an eye on the app in early December for winner notices.

Open the app this week, tap the consent button, and let your next market run count.
