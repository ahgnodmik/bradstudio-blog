If your photos, videos and backups are spread across three external drives and a cloud plan you keep paying for, Minisforum's new N5 Air 7640 is built to pull them into one box.

Some social posts called it a mini PC. It is not. It is a five-bay network attached storage (NAS) unit. Minisforum's own store and the tech press both list it as a NAS. The barebone version starts at $495. The US store lists shipping for late October 2026. Below are the facts that at least two sources agree on.

What is new

The original N5 Air launched earlier this year with an AMD Ryzen 7 255. The N5 Air 7640 is a cheaper variant with a different chip. It uses the Ryzen 5 7640HS. That is a Zen 4 processor with 6 cores and 12 threads and Radeon 760M integrated graphics. Notebookcheck described it as a quietly launched, cheaper N5 Air.

The design detail that stands out is the motherboard. It sits on a tray that slides out of the case. Minisforum says this gives quick access to the memory and SSD slots. Yanko Design and Notebookcheck both highlighted the slide-out layout. A LocalHake review of the earlier N5 Air found you remove screws first before the board slides out. So expect a screwdriver, not a fully tool-free job.

Specs that sources agree on

  • Drive bays: five SATA bays for 2.5-inch or 3.5-inch drives
  • Fast storage: three M.2 NVMe slots
  • Networking: one 10GbE port and one 5GbE port
  • Expansion: one OCuLink port, two USB4 ports and one PCIe x16 slot
  • Memory: two DDR5 SO-DIMM laptop memory slots
  • CPU: Ryzen 5 7640HS, 6 cores, 12 threads

Where the capacity numbers differ

Notebookcheck and Mezha list a maximum of 174TB. The Minisforum store lists 150TB for the five hard drives. Borncity explains the gap. Five drives of up to 30TB each give 150TB. The three M.2 slots add up to 24TB more. That makes 174TB. So both figures are right; one counts the SSDs and one does not.

Memory support is reported as up to 96GB of DDR5. That is the figure for the N5 Air family. Check the official spec sheet for the 7640 before you buy large memory kits.

How it differs from a typical home NAS

Most home NAS boxes use a low-power chip with 1GbE or 2.5GbE networking. The N5 Air 7640 starts with a laptop-class Ryzen and 10GbE. That matters if your desktop or switch also runs at 10GbE. Large video files move far faster than over a standard gigabit link.

The PCIe slot and OCuLink port leave room to grow. Minisforum says the PCIe slot can take a network card or an SSD cache array. That makes the box a candidate for a small home server too. Think photo backup, media streaming and a few virtual machines on the same hardware.

Do not buy it for local AI. The product name says "AI NAS." Notebookcheck noted that the 7640HS's integrated graphics and NPU are not strong. If running AI models at home is your main goal, look at a machine built for that job.

The $495 barebone price comes from the Minisforum store and several reports. One Notebookcheck article put the 16GB model at $495 too. Borncity and Notebookcheck's Italian edition list $679. Treat $679 as the likely figure and confirm it at checkout. The store shows $495 as a sale price, so it may not last.

For comparison, the Ryzen 7 255 version of the N5 Air sells for $535 barebone in the US. The 7640 saves about $40 in exchange for the older, slower chip.

No configuration includes hard drives. Fill all five bays and the drives can cost more than the NAS itself. Budget for that before you compare it with a NAS sold with disks.

Before you order

  • Check your network. The 10GbE port only pays off if your switch and computer support fast networking.
  • Price the drives. Decide how many bays you will fill on day one.
  • Confirm the RAM price. The 16GB price differs between reports.
  • Check local shipping and warranty. Ship dates for regions outside the US were not published at the time of writing. If you import the unit, add duties and ask the seller how warranty repairs work in your country.

What is not covered here: the operating system, exact dimensions and weight, and power draw. We found these in only one source each, so they are left out.
