If you live in Korea and plan to order from Amazon, Best Buy or a European shop this Black Friday, the price at checkout may not be the price you pay. Korea's duty-free limit for overseas direct purchases decides whether your parcel arrives tax-free or with a customs bill, and the rules are stricter than many foreign residents expect. This guide covers the USD 200 and USD 150 thresholds, the exceptions for health supplements and cosmetics, the combined-taxation rule that catches split orders, and the Personal Customs Clearance Code you need before you click "buy".

Black Friday 2026 dates for shoppers in Korea

US Thanksgiving falls on Thursday 26 November 2026, so Black Friday is Friday 27 November and Cyber Monday is Monday 30 November. Because Korea is 14 hours ahead of US Eastern Time in winter, many US "midnight" deals start on Friday afternoon in Seoul. Shipping peaks after these sales too, so allow extra time for anything you need by Christmas.

Korea's duty-free limit: USD 200 from the US, USD 150 elsewhere

Korea lets individuals import goods for personal use without duty or VAT up to a set value. The limit depends on where the item ships from and what it is.

ShipmentItem typeDuty-free limit
Shipped from the United States, simple list clearanceGeneral goods (clothes, electronics, shoes, toys and so on)USD 200
Shipped from any other country, simple list clearanceGeneral goodsUSD 150
Any country, including the USHealth functional foods (supplements), medicines, functional cosmetics and other items excluded from list clearanceUSD 150

The US gets a higher limit for general goods under list clearance, the simplified process Korea Customs Service uses for small personal parcels. Korean tax press (Korea Tax Times) and consumer guides from the Ministry of Government Legislation's Easylaw service describe the same split. These limits are set in US dollars. Do not rely on a fixed won figure: the won amount moves with the exchange rate, and customs applies its own official exchange rate.

Exceptions: health supplements and cosmetics

This is where most expats get caught. Some categories are excluded from list clearance even when they ship from the US. These goods must go through a formal import declaration, and the limit drops to USD 150 regardless of origin.

  • Health supplements. Vitamins, omega-3, probiotics and similar products are excluded from list clearance. Personal-use quantity is also capped: up to six bottles per person is generally accepted as personal use, according to Easylaw and Korea Tax Times. Going over that count can mean the goods are not treated as personal use at all.
  • Medicines. Over-the-counter drugs bought abroad follow the same USD 150 limit and face extra checks. Some ingredients legal in the US are restricted in Korea.
  • Cosmetics. Functional cosmetics, the Korean category covering products such as sunscreen, whitening and anti-wrinkle items, are excluded from list clearance and fall under USD 150. Guides disagree on how ordinary make-up and skincare are treated, so treat any cosmetics order from the US as a USD 150 order to be safe.

Practical point: a US shopping cart with a jacket and a tub of protein powder or a bottle of vitamins can be split into two clearance tracks. Read the product category, not only the price.

What counts toward the limit, and what happens if you go over

The value customs checks is the goods price. Korean guides, including reporting in Vegan News and explanations from customs brokers, say this includes any sales tax charged in the seller's country and shipping inside that country. International shipping to Korea is generally left out when it is clearly listed separately.

Going over the limit has a sharp edge. If the value exceeds the threshold by even one dollar, duty and VAT apply to the whole amount, not just the excess. Once a parcel becomes taxable, the taxable value usually includes international freight and insurance too. Korean VAT on imports is 10%, charged on the value plus duty. Duty rates vary by product.

Example, in dollars only: a USD 190 coat from a US store clears free. The same coat at USD 205 is taxed on the full value plus shipping, not on USD 5.

Combined taxation: the rule that catches split orders

Many shoppers try to stay under the limit by splitting one big order into several small ones. Korea's combined taxation rule (합산과세) is designed to stop that.

SituationTreatment
Same seller, same purchase date, arriving in separate boxesValues added together and taxed as one
Several orders consolidated into one box by a forwarding serviceOne shipment, taxed on the combined value
Same seller, different purchase datesAssessed separately
Different sellers, same dayAssessed separately

Before 17 November 2022, two parcels that simply arrived at a Korean port on the same day could be combined. Korea Customs Service removed that arrival-date test from its import clearance notice, so parcels bought on different days, or from different sellers, are no longer combined just because they land together. The Fact and the Korea International Trade Association both reported the change.

Watch the marketplace trap. On Amazon or eBay, "same seller" means the actual merchant, not the platform. Two items from the same third-party merchant on the same day can be combined. Items from two different merchants usually are not.

Personal Customs Clearance Code: get it, and renew it, before you shop

Every personal import into Korea needs an identifier. For most people that is the Personal Customs Clearance Code (PCCC): the letter P followed by 12 digits, issued free by Korea Customs Service.

How foreign residents get a code

  • Online. Apply on the UNI-PASS website or the Korea Customs Service mobile app. You need an Alien Registration Card (or a Certificate of Foreign Residence) plus a Korean mobile phone in your name or a digital certificate, according to the Korea Customs Service English FAQ.
  • In person. If online identity checks fail, which happens with some foreign-issued phone contracts, you can apply at a customs office with your Alien Registration Card.
  • Without a code. The Korea Customs Service FAQ and FedEx's Korea guidance both say foreigners can declare using an alien registration number or passport number instead. A PCCC is still the smoother option for frequent shoppers.

New for 2026: the code now expires

From 2026, Personal Customs Clearance Codes are valid for one year. Codes issued in 2026 or later expire one year after issue. Codes issued before 2026 expire on the holder's birthday in 2027. If you do not renew within 30 days either side of the expiry date, the code is cancelled automatically, according to the government portal Korea.kr, Law Times and iNews24. Updating your details or reissuing the code resets the one-year clock.

The name on your code must match the recipient name on your order exactly. If your Alien Registration Card spells your name in a particular order, use that order on Amazon and with your forwarder.

Black Friday direct purchase checklist

  • Check your PCCC on UNI-PASS today. Confirm it is active and the phone number is current.
  • Write the code into the customs ID field of each shopping account, with your name exactly as registered.
  • Sort your cart by origin: US general goods (USD 200), everything else (USD 150).
  • Pull supplements, medicines and cosmetics into separate orders and keep each under USD 150.
  • Do not split one seller's items across orders on the same day; that is combined anyway.
  • Keep receipts and order confirmations. You will need them if customs asks, or if you return an item and claim back duty.
  • Use the Korea Customs Service expected-tax lookup on UNI-PASS if an order is near the limit.
